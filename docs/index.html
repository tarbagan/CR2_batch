CR2 Batch

Batch process CR2 and other photo files, apply Adobe XMP presets, and render RAW to JPG through Adobe Photoshop for output that stays aligned with the Adobe workflow.

Select XMP presets created in Adobe Photoshop or Camera Raw and apply them in a compatible workflow.

Photoshop Render

RAW rendering uses Adobe Photoshop automation instead of trying to approximate the Adobe engine.
